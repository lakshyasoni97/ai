Selected work

Multi-agent customer support

Deloitte

An intent router hands each support ticket to specialised agents — one retrieves policy documents, one calls internal APIs and databases, one checks the draft reply against policy — before a response goes back to the customer. Built with Google ADK on Vertex AI Agent Engine, with Pub/Sub for the event plumbing. Resolution time went from one to two days to near-instant for the ticket types it covers, an estimated $1.3M a year in saved support cost.

Google ADK, Gemini, Vertex AI, Cloud Pub/Sub

Internal knowledge assistant

Deloitte

A chat interface over years of slide decks and PDFs, so consultants can ask "have we pitched this before?" and get the actual slides back with citations. LangGraph for the retrieval flow, ChromaDB for the index.

LangGraph, LangChain, ChromaDB, Streamlit

Video clip repurposing

Network18

Takes a broadcast transcript, asks Gemini to score segments for how well they'd stand alone, and cuts the best ones into short clips automatically. Editors went from scrubbing footage to reviewing a shortlist — roughly 85% less manual editing.

Gemini API, FastAPI, MoviePy, Docker

Reporting automation

Network18

Replaced a set of hand-assembled weekly reports with scheduled pipelines that scrape, clean and load data into BigQuery and feed Power BI dashboards. Saves about 3,500 hours of manual work a year across departments.

Python, Selenium, BigQuery, Power BI

Market news trend tracker

Network18

Reads 600+ financial news articles a day, extracts companies and people with NER, and clusters coverage to show which stories are picking up momentum.

Transformers, NER, TF-IDF, K-Means, LDA
